## 3. Decisions the user took (re-confirm or drop at the restart)

- **Grid and closure:** two moments, the EA closure, 8 bins (22 November, re-confirmed after the collide fix).
- **Carbon material:** THEMIS a-C(:H) at rho 1.6 by default, graphite as the control (22 November).
- **Grain hydrogen:** the equilibrium band gap, nothing tracked by default; the M_H block takes 0-4 bins at compile
  time (22 November).
- **Carbon mass transfer** (23 November):
  - no PAH clusters;
  - molecule + molecule has no outcome;
  - molecule + larger particle goes by impact speed (labelled): stick below ~4 km/s, rebound between, destroyed above
    ~7 km/s;
  - sticking times S_eff = 1/(1 + R_abs (P_th + Y_pd) t_bury), with Y_pd a labelled calibrated yield (default 0);
  - the hydrocarbon-family identity below N_C 400;
  - one labelled seam law for the electron sticking.
- **Photodesorption on every host** (24 November): "the photodesorption should consistently act across the whole
  population".
- **A3c details** (23 November):
  - an exploding grain's mass goes to a^-3.3 fragments;
  - Track O targets G23 plus the HD23 model.
- **PAH hydrogen** (24 November): equilibrium per cell for all PAH bins, nothing tracked, no precomputed hydrogen
  tables. Charging, hydrogenation, H2, PE heating, recombination, photodissociation and fragmentation are all taken
  from the local gas and the RT field. The study's errors are accepted.
  - This supersedes the tables decision of 15 November.
  - It also supersedes the same day's choice to track the 9 class populations in bins 1-2.
- **Charge cap +5** for the PAHrt kernels (25 November): provisional, "we'll have to revisit for sure".
- **Expensive computations go to megatron** (24 November). The uncapped bins 3-4 build was stopped on 25 November as
  too expensive.

   - 2-3 molecules per bin;
   - two cadences (a charge-only balance every substep, the full solve at a lower cadence);
   - a cheap live-state predictor.
2. **The multiphoton proxy:** self (default) or power, which bracket the truth; or a labelled midpoint.

4. **The charge cap,** to revisit once pahchem fixes its ladder extension (section 5).

   - Selective C2H2 loss never depletes the catacondensed share. Coupled as is, a bin would lose carbon at the fragile
     molecules' rate for ever.
   - Options: a per-structure carbon output with a tracked share, or compact-only bins.
6. **Cosmic rays, hot gas and C 1s** are not in the bases yet (T3).

## 5. Known bugs and issues (not fixed)

**In this line:**
- **The `gsd.charging` cache key** (`_table_path`) hashes only the photon groups, U_GRID and
  `rt_group_charging.FORMAT_VERSION`. A physics change in `build_bin_table` therefore never invalidates cached
  tables.
  - Two caches already had the same file names with different arrays (40 of 47 files).
  - The hub now holds the cache the results used. The older one is in `_archive/gsd_charging_hub_2026-10-16`.
- **Stale text:**
  - the report and `STATUS.md` still say photo-release acts on nano-grains only;
  - `docs/api/gsd.md:183` on main says 4.4 % (now 7.1 %).
- **Python cost of the operator:** ~165 ms per precool and 25-420 ms per update in the one-zone demo. RAMSES writes
  its own kernels.

**In pahchem** (to fix in `../pyCALIMA-pahchem`; they reach this line through main):
- **A round-off guard:** P(H) per deposit came out 1 + 1.4e-12. This killed the hot-gas table run `hot_k8`.
- **`extend_ladder`** repeats the last computed ionisation step. PAHdb mixes RB3LYP and UB3LYP between charge states,
  so for some molecules that step is far off: C89H27 reaches +9 below 13.6 eV, and C115H29 and C121H29 reach +15/+16.
  This caused S1's C89H27 <Z> +6.8. A fix is proposed (the fitted step where the last one is more than 25 % off it;
  28 molecules) and awaits the user.
- **Old-star optical light** is scaled with every G0 node of the multiphoton tables, which extrapolate below G0 1.
  This is behind the C268H52 crash at G0 0.01. The fix is pending. The PAHrt bases carry no old-star light.
- **`_threshold_factors`** gives per-N_H ionisation shifts as ratios per field shape, and 1 where the intact state
  has no ionisation. Band-split fields therefore lose the stripped states' ionisation. An absolute accessor is needed.
- **`beyer_swinehart` DOS** is recomputed about 808 times per field shape. A memo would cut the kernel builds.
- **A hard 1-100 eV field at G0 >= 1e4** puts deposits and H2 off by up to 3 dex in S6's V1. Unexplained.

**Legacy and side findings:** the list in `STATUS.md` §5.

## 6. What the work taught (worth carrying into the restart)

1. **The field shape matters as much as G0** (S1).
   - At fixed G0, the in-group shape moves PAH carbon loss by 4.7-8 dex, H2 by up to 2.3 dex and heating by 0.7-1
     dex.
   - So tables on (G0, n_H, T, x_e) cannot hold PAH photochemistry: anything that does must read the RT group
     spectrum.
   - RAMSES's group mean energies are global, though, so the in-group shape inside a cell is not known.
2. **The charge relaxes much faster than the hydrogenation** (S7). With the charge in equilibrium per N_H, the error
   stays within a few %. Pure equilibrium fails for H2 in fluctuating gas: time-averaged x100-260 in bins 1-2.
3. **Averaging rates over molecules breaks hydrogenation** (S2). The mean-of-rates molecule strips like the most
   fragile one, so carbon loss comes out x100-1e7 off. Solving representative molecules separately avoids this, but
   it fixes the structural mix (section 4, item 5).
4. **Per-state single-photon rates are exactly linear in the field.** The only non-linearity is multiphoton heating
   (G0^2 dehydrogenation for N_C >~ 50, G0^2-3 bare carbon loss).
5. **State counts drive the cost.**
   - Making every charge live pulled in pahchem's cold-limit ladder: C89H27 had charges -1..+17 and 608 states.
   - The +5 cap brings it to 224 states, and the solve from 9e5 to 5e4 flops.
   - Only 35-85 states are populated per molecule.
   - Building kernels for C268H52 takes 42-79 s per field shape capped, against 13-19 min uncapped.
6. **At 8 bins, thermal release from hosts is about 0.** The smallest host is 1.54 nm against molecules of <N_C> 35,
   so freeze-out is set by the photodesorption yield. A dense core freezes out at any yield.
7. **Practice:**
   - plot every fit: O2's first fit had sawtooth size distributions that showed only once plotted;
   - rerun the GSD checks after each merge of main, since pahchem drift once moved the seam unnoticed for 2 weeks;
   - cache keys must hash the physics (section 5);
   - estimate memory with 1 worker before a parallel build (pahchem workers hold 1.4-6.5 GB each for large
     molecules).

# a-C(:H) instead of graphite, and the N_C ~ 300–400 molecular limit: verified references

Literature check by a Sonnet subagent, 2026-11-17, for the GSD overview (point 1 of the user's review). Saved by the
main session. "Verified" = read on the arXiv abstract page or full text; "secondhand" = only in a search summary or a
citing paper; "inference" = the subagent's reasoning. Arguments marked secondhand must be checked before citing.

## Key quotes and facts

- **Draine 2003** (ARA&A 41, 241; astro-ph/0304489), full text. §3.3: "the graphite hypothesis does not appear to have a
  natural way to accomodate the observed variations in FWHM of the profile while at the same time holding λ0 nearly
  constant ... (Draine & Malhotra 1993)". §3.4: the 3.4 µm band is "generally attributed to the aliphatic C-H stretch
  (Sandford et al. 1991)"; its abundance cannot be determined from the band. §8: the provisional model "does not include
  a component to reproduce the observed 3.4 µm absorption feature". Table 3: presolar graphite ~10⁻⁶ of meteorite mass,
  survivor-biased.
- **Jäger+09** (ApJ 696, 706; 0903.0775): gas-phase condensation gives PAH-built soot below ~1700 K and fullerene-like
  grains above ~3500 K.
- **Jones+17** (A&A 602, A46; 1703.00775) §2.1.3: "there appears to be no evidence to support it [graphite] as the most
  abundant interstellar carbon dust material". Table 1: densities 1.60 (a-C nanoparticles), 1.51–1.57 (large a-C:H/a-C).
  §5.1: "The presence of an a-C:H mantle on a-Sil dust is inconsistent with observations" (unpolarised 3.4 µm band).
- **Planck Int. XXIX** (A&A 586, A132; 1409.2495): "The DL Av estimates are larger than those determined towards QSOs by
  a factor of about 2, which depends on Umin."
- **Fanciullo+15** (A&A 580, A136; 1506.07011): "the best accord between model and observation is obtained with the more
  emissive grains derived from recent laboratory data on silicates and amorphous carbons."
- **Chiar+06** (astro-ph/0607245, secondhand): no 3.4 µm polarisation where the 9.7 µm band is polarised.
- **Guillet+18** (A&A 610, A16; 1710.04598, secondhand) and **Planck Int. XXI** (A&A 576, A106; 1405.0873,
  secondhand): P353/p_V underpredicted by pre-Planck models by ~2.5.
- **Hensley & Draine 2023** (ApJ 948, 55; 2208.12365): a single astrodust composition for a ≳ 0.02 µm best explains the
  FIR polarisation; PAHs below ~1000 C; "turbostratic" graphite blended into the carbonaceous cross sections.
- **Jones+13** (A&A 558, A62; 1411.6293): a-C:H H-rich (15–60 at.%), a-C H-poor (≤ 15 at.%); small grains maximally
  aromatised in the diffuse ISM; large a-C(:H) grains (≥ 200 nm) stay aliphatic-rich.
- **Köhler+15** (1506.01533): an accreted aliphatic mantle raises the FIR opacity ~2× (β 1.5 → 1.8).
- **Croiset+16** (A&A 590, A26; 1603.02577): N_C ≈ 70 in the NGC 7023 cavity, ≈ 50 at the PDR surface (11.2/3.3 with
  PAHdb).
- **Draine+21** (ApJ 917, 3; 2011.07046) §V.1: "only the smallest PAH sizes (N_C ≲ 100) radiate appreciably in the
  3.3 µm feature"; "The strong 7.7 µm feature is efficiently radiated by PAHs with N_C ~ 10²"; "the 17 µm feature is
  efficiently radiated by large PAHs, with N_C ≳ 10³".
- **Ricca+12** (ApJ 754, 75; no arXiv, abstract via search): above 150 C "the 6.3 µm feature becomes very broad and
  shifts to longer wavelengths, and the 8.6 µm band becomes stronger than the '7.7' µm band"; PAHs of ≥ 150 C "are
  unlikely to be the dominant species in interstellar space".
- **Boersma+10** (A&A 511, A32): compact PAHs of 50–130 C show the 17.4 µm band; unclear above ~200 C.
- **Rapacioli+06** (A&A 460, 519; astro-ph/0609186): "A minimal size of 400 carbon atoms per cluster was inferred"
  (Rapacioli+05, A&A 429, 193). **Berné+07** (astro-ph/0703072): VSG and PAH populations. **Pilleri+15** (A&A 577,
  A16; 1502.04941): eVSGs of mixed aromatic and aliphatic composition; their photo-processing gives PAHs with
  aliphatic side groups.
- **Montillaud+13** (A&A 552, A15; 1301.6507): "PAHs smaller than 50 carbon atoms are not expected to survive in the
  NGC 7023 NW PDR", similar for the diffuse ISM. **Murga+22** (MNRAS 509, 800; 2111.05583): C loss limited to N_C < 60.
- **Draine & Li 2007** (ApJ 657, 810; astro-ph/0608003): N_C = 460 (a/10 Å)³; the PAH-to-graphite transition at 50 Å
  is "entirely ad-hoc"; PAHs of 15–40 Å make the 24 µm continuum.

Not found or not verified: "Lemmens et al. 2023" (nearest: Sidhu+23, 2304.11230); WR/SN carbon condensation; Robertson
1986; the 2175 Å bump polarisation; Andrews+15 N_C values (Andrews+16 exists, A&A 595, A23, read locally by the other
review).

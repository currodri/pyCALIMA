Recommended rule (agent's inference): stick below ~1 eV per atom (physisorption trapping; flat PAH ~0.07 eV per C);
rebound intact between ~1 and ~3-4 eV per atom; destroyed above E_th ~ 3 eV per atom (factor ~2), by C2 loss then
multifragmentation; ~30x the bulk Q*_D (0.1 eV per atom). Per-atom scaling (cluster literature) makes the threshold
velocity size-independent: v = sqrt(2 E_th / m_C) ~ 7 km/s (5-10 km/s for 1.5-6 eV per atom); sticking ends at ~4 km/s.
